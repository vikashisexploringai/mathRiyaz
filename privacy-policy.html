Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 07, 2026

1. Overview

This privacy policy explains how our Quiz App handles user data. The app is designed to provide educational quiz content through a simple interface.

2. No Data Collection

The Quiz App does not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No location data or GPS information
  • No photos, camera access, or media files
  • No microphone or audio recordings
  • No contacts or phone information
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No usage statistics or analytics
  • No cookies or tracking technologies

3. App Permissions

The app requests only one permission:

  • Internet access: Required solely to load quiz content from GitHub Pages. No other permissions are requested or used.

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. The quiz content is hosted on GitHub Pages, but GitHub does not collect data through this app.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for users of all ages. Since no data is collected, there is no risk of children's data being gathered or stored.

6. Data Security

As no data is collected, there are no data storage or security concerns. The app simply displays web content with no data processing.

7. Changes to This Policy
